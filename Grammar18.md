# Grammar: so...wie / genauso...wie / nicht so...wie vs. Komparativ + als

### 1. Equality comparisons — wie

| Construction | Meaning | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| so + Adj/Adv + wie | as...as (equal) | Er ist so groß wie sein Bruder. | He is as tall as his brother. |
| genauso + Adj/Adv + wie | just as...as (emphasized, exact equality) | Er ist genauso groß wie sein Bruder. | He is just as tall as his brother. |
| ebenso + Adj/Adv + wie | equally...as (synonym of genauso, slightly more formal) | Sie ist ebenso klug wie ihre Schwester. | She is equally as smart as her sister. |
| nicht so + Adj/Adv + wie | not as...as (the first item comes up short) | Er ist nicht so groß wie sein Bruder. | He is not as tall as his brother. |

With adverbs, same pattern:

| Er läuft so schnell wie ich. | He runs as fast as I do. |
| --- | --- |
| Er läuft nicht so schnell wie ich. | He doesn't run as fast as I do. |

---

### 2. Inequality comparisons — Komparativ + als

**Structure:** Adjektiv/Adverb + **-er** + als

1. Er ist größer als sein Bruder. (He is taller than his brother.)
2. Sie läuft schneller als ich. (She runs faster than me.)
3. Das schmeckt besser als das andere. (This tastes better than the other one — irregular comparatives like besser/mehr/lieber follow the same als rule.)

> ⚠️ **Trap:** never say "größer **wie**" — that's a common colloquial error (especially southern German/Austrian speech). A comparative (-er form) is always paired with **als**, never wie.

---

### 3. als vs. wie — the core rule

| Use... | When... | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **wie** | comparing two things that are **equal or similar** (so...wie, genauso...wie), or saying something is "like" something else | Er sieht aus wie sein Vater. (He looks like his father.) |
| **als** | comparing two things that are **unequal** — after a comparative (-er) form | Er ist stärker als sein Vater. (He is stronger than his father.) |

als has two other jobs that are unrelated to comparison — worth knowing so you don't mix them up: **als** = "when" for a single completed event in the past ("Als ich ankam, regnete es." — When I arrived, it was raining.), and **als** = "as/in the role of" ("Sie arbeitet als Lehrerin." — She works as a teacher.). Neither of those is a comparison — only the comparative-degree usage pairs with an -er adjective.

---

### 4. Quick reference

| Type | Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Equal | so + Adj + wie | Er ist so alt wie ich. | He is as old as I am. |
| Emphasized equal | genauso + Adj + wie | Er ist genauso alt wie ich. | He is just as old as I am. |
| Unequal, negative | nicht so + Adj + wie | Er ist nicht so alt wie ich. | He is not as old as I am. |
| Unequal, comparative | Komparativ + als | Er ist älter als ich. | He is older than I am. |
